Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much is a digital wedding invitation?

From $119 with Classic. Spark costs $179 and Grand Moment, $239. Grand Moment is the most complete for weddings: family pass, downloadable guest list, dietary restrictions and save the date.

Which package is right for my wedding?

If you want the essentials with photos, Classic. If you want music, an itinerary and RSVP on the page, Spark. If you have many guests and want to control seats and menus, Grand Moment.

What is the family pass?

Each family sees in their invitation how many spots are reserved for them. That avoids surprise guests and improves your headcount. It's in Grand Moment.

Do you include lodging or transportation for out-of-town guests?

We can include a section with information about lodging, transportation and other services for your out-of-town guests. It is only information about independent vendors: Moment0 does not hire them and is not responsible for their services.

Do you make invitations for destination weddings?

Yes. Because it's digital, we make invitations for weddings anywhere in Mexico, the United States or the world, and Spark and Grand Moment include an English or bilingual version.

How far ahead should I order it?

We recommend ordering 2 to 3 months before your wedding. Delivery takes 15 business days from the moment we have all your information.

How long is it active?

6 months in Spark and Grand Moment, and 3 months in Classic, counted from the moment you approve your invitation.
