Frequently asked questions

Everything you might be wondering

How long does my invitation take?

15 business days from the moment we have all your information: details, photos and text.

How far ahead should I order it?

We recommend ordering at least 6 weeks before your event, or 2 to 3 months ahead for a wedding or quinceañera. That way it's ready without a rush, and your guests get it with time to plan.

Do you take the photos?

No, you send us the photos you want to use. We recommend clear, well-lit photos without filters. To keep them from losing quality, send them on WhatsApp as a document, by email, or upload them to the Drive folder we share with you.

Can you recommend a photographer?

Yes. If your event is in Mexico City or the State of Mexico, we're happy to share contacts for photographers we trust.

How many changes can I ask for?

It depends on your package: Essential includes 1 round of changes, Classic 2, Spark 2 and Grand Moment 3. A round is when you send us all your changes together in one message. Each extra round costs $6.

What if a detail changes after delivery, like the time or the venue?

We update it for $6 per change, and your same link updates, so you don't have to send it again.

How do I pay?

You can pay online. We don't accept bank transfers for US dollar payments. Payment is 100% upfront, before we start your invitation. Prices are in US dollars.

Do prices include tax?

Yes, all our prices already include IVA (Mexican VAT). If you need an invoice, request it when you make your payment.

Can I upgrade my package after ordering?

Yes, by paying the difference. We recommend choosing your package from the start, because we plan each invitation ahead to deliver it on time. If your plans change, we're happy to adjust.

Do you make invitations for events anywhere?

Yes. Because it's digital, we make invitations for events across Mexico, the United States and anywhere in the world.

Can you make my invitation in English or bilingual?

Yes, it's available in the Spark and Grand Moment packages.

How do I share it?

We give you a link you can send by WhatsApp, social media or email.

Do my guests need to download anything?

No, the invitation opens right in their phone's browser.

How do my guests RSVP?

In Essential and Classic, with a button that takes them to your WhatsApp. In Spark and Grand Moment, with a button on the invitation that opens a form.

How long is my invitation active?

3 months in Essential and Classic, and 6 months in Spark and Grand Moment, counted from the moment you approve your invitation.

Can I use a character in my invitation?

We design your invitation in the theme you choose, with our own illustrations inspired by it. If the theme is a third-party character or brand, using it is the client's responsibility.
