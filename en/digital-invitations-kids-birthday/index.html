Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much is a digital kids' birthday invitation?

From $50 with the Essential package: a design in the theme you choose, countdown, directions and RSVP on WhatsApp. With photos and a gift registry link, Classic costs $100.

Can I choose the party theme?

Yes, the design follows the theme you choose. Using third-party characters, brands or images is the client's full responsibility.

How long does my invitation take?

15 business days from the moment we have all your information: details, photos and text. We recommend ordering at least 6 weeks before the party.

How do my guests receive it?

We send you the link to your invitation and you share it on WhatsApp. It opens on their phone, with nothing to download.

How long is it active?

3 months in Essential and Classic, counted from the moment you approve your invitation.

How do I pay?

You can pay online or by bank transfer. Payment is 100% upfront, before we start your invitation. Prices are in US dollars.
