Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much is a digital First Communion invitation?

From $50 with the Essential package: a design in the theme you choose, countdown, directions and RSVP on WhatsApp. Classic costs $100 and adds parents and godparents, a quote or Bible verse, up to 5 photos and a gift registry link. Spark costs $150.

Can I include a Bible verse and the godparents?

Yes, from the Classic package you can include a special quote or Bible verse, plus the parents and godparents.

Can I list the time of the Mass and the celebration?

Yes. With Spark we build an itinerary with each moment, like the Mass and the lunch. With Essential and Classic we list the date, time and place of your event.

How far ahead should I order it?

We recommend ordering at least 6 weeks before the First Communion. Delivery takes 15 business days from the moment we have all your information.

How do my guests receive it?

We send you the link to your invitation and you share it on WhatsApp. It opens on their phone, with nothing to download.

How long is it active?

3 months in Essential and Classic, and 6 months in Spark, counted from the moment you approve your invitation.
