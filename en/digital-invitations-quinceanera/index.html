Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much is a digital quinceañera invitation?

From $100 with Classic. Spark, the most popular, costs $150 and includes up to 15 photos, music, an itinerary and RSVP on the page. Grand Moment costs $200.

Can I add music?

Yes, Spark and Grand Moment include background music. The client is fully responsible for music rights.

How many photos can I include?

Up to 5 in Classic, up to 15 in Spark, and unlimited photos plus video in Grand Moment.

Can the invitation be bilingual?

Yes. Spark and Grand Moment include an English or bilingual (English/Spanish) version, so every guest can follow along.

How do guests RSVP?

In Spark and Grand Moment, they RSVP with a form inside the invitation. With Grand Moment you can download the guest list.

How far ahead should I order it?

We recommend ordering 2 to 3 months before the quinceañera. Delivery takes 15 business days from the moment we have all your information.

How long is it active?

6 months in Spark and Grand Moment, and 3 months in Classic, counted from the moment you approve your invitation.
